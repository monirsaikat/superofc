# Income tax

SuperOfc works out monthly income tax from a yearly projection and evens out the total in the last month of the fiscal year. A one-off bonus is counted once, not for every remaining month.

## Rules
On **Tax › Rules**, choose the fiscal year and either **Start from pack** or **Enter rules**. The rules are:

- **Slabs:** yearly income bands with a rate each. Leave the width of the last band empty.
- **Categories:** a larger tax-free first slab for, for example, women or senior citizens.
- **Exemptions:** a fixed amount, or a share of income or of one component, with an optional cap.
- **Investment rebate, minimum tax and rounding.**

Use **Quick check** to see the yearly tax for any income before saving. Once finalized payroll has used the rules, saving a change adds a new version from the next open month, so past payslips never change.

> SuperOfc ships with a sample pack that is not any country's real law. Enter or check your country's rules before relying on the figures.

## Employee declarations
On **Tax › Employee declarations**, set each employee's category, declared investment, income and tax from a previous employer this year, and whether they are exempt. Select **Save**.

## Statements
The employee's **Tax** tab shows this month's tax and how it is worked out. **Tax › Tax deducted** lists the year per employee. Save a statement PDF per employee, or the whole summary to Excel.
