# Getting started

## First run
The first time SuperOfc opens, a short wizard asks for:

- **Company:** name, currency and how numbers are grouped (1,234,567 or 12,34,567).
- **Calendar:** time zone, the month your fiscal year starts and the date format.
- **Working week:** which days are off. You can change this later.
- **Administrator:** your user name and a password of at least 8 characters. Keep it safe; there is no reset by e-mail.

## Signing in and locking
Sign in with your user name and password. After several wrong passwords the account is locked for a few minutes.
Press Ctrl+L, or leave the PC idle, and the screen locks. Your password opens it again where you left off.

## Users and roles
Add users under **Settings › Users**. Each user has one role:

| Role | Can do |
|------|--------|
| Admin | Everything, including users, backups and restore. |
| HR | Employees, attendance, leave, devices, loan requests and salary changes. |
| Accounts | Payroll, loans, tax and salary reports. |
| Viewer | Look at employees, attendance and reports without salaries; change nothing. |

Salary, payroll, loan and tax pages are hidden from roles that cannot see salaries. Every change is recorded in **Settings › Audit log** with who made it and when.
