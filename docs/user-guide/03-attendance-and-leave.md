# Attendance and leave

## Shifts
Under **Settings › Shifts** set start and end times, grace minutes, break, and the minimum hours for a half or full day. Night shifts can end after midnight. Assign shifts per employee or to a whole department; rotations are supported.

## The monthly sheet
**Attendance** shows every employee and day in colour: present, late, half day, absent, leave, holiday and weekly off, with totals on the right. Select a day to see its punches.

Days are worked out from punches automatically. If a punch is missing or wrong, select the day and add a correction with a reason; you can undo it later. Manual punches always need a reason and are marked as manual.

## Leave
Leave types such as casual and sick leave are set under **Settings › Leave types**, with yearly or monthly credit and how much carries over to the next year.

On **Leave**, create a request for an employee. HR or an approver approves or rejects it; the balance changes only when it is approved. Cancelling an approved request gives the days back.

## Locked months
Once a month's payroll is finalized, attendance, leave and salary changes for those dates are blocked, and a banner says so. To change them, void that payroll run first (see [Payroll](05-payroll.md)).
